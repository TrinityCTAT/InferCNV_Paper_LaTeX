\section*{Availability of data and materials}\label{sec:availability}

inferCNV is an R package released under the BSD 3-clause licence. It is available from Bioconductor (https://bioconductor.org/packages/infercnv) \citep{infercnvBioc} and GitHub (https://github.com/broadinstitute/infercnv), with documentation on the GitHub wiki (https://github.com/broadinstitute/infercnv/wiki). A Docker image with all dependencies is available at https://hub.docker.com/r/trinityctat/infercnv. Example data sets with run scripts are at https://github.com/broadinstitute/inferCNV\_examples, and a tutorial is at https://github.com/broadinstitute/infercnv\_workshop \todo{confirm public URL}. This article describes release \todo{version to be set by the maintainer}.

Scripts for the analyses in this article are available at https://github.com/TrinityCTAT/InferCNV\_Paper \todo{confirm public before posting}. The simulation toolkit is at https://github.com/broadinstitute/inferCNV\_benchmarking.

All data analysed in this study are publicly available, except the raw Smart-seq2 reads used for a cross-check, described below. Oligodendroglioma and glioblastoma Smart-seq2 data (Figs.~\ref{fig:1}--\ref{fig:3}) are from the inferCNV package and the inferCNV\_examples repository \citep{tirosh2016oligo, patel2014}. The 10x PBMC 3k and TNBC1 matrices and the BCH869 clone labels are from the xclone-data repository \citep{tenx2016pbmc3k, xclonedata}; MM199 counts are from GEO GSE141299, BCC06 and BCC06post counts from GEO GSE123813, and their whole-exome copy number segments from the Zenodo deposit of \citet{schmid2025zenodoTruth}. The breast cancer atlas used for scaling is GEO GSE176078 \citep{wu2021breast}, and the colorectal cancer atlas used for simulation parameters is GEO GSE178341 \citep{pelka2021}. The H3K27M glioma and synovial sarcoma analyses used the expression matrices, cell annotations and fusion calls deposited by the original studies (GEO GSE102130 and GSE131309; Single Cell Portal SCP147 and SCP167) \citep{filbin2018, jerbyarnon2021}. The raw Smart-seq2 reads of these tumours, which we realigned only for the cross-check described in Methods, were generated at the Broad Institute in those studies, to which members of our group contributed, and were analysed from the Broad Institute's copies. They were not deposited in public archives because of patient privacy: the raw reads of the synovial sarcomas are available under controlled access through DUOS (GEO GSE131309), and, as stated in GEO GSE102130, the raw reads of the H3K27M gliomas could not be deposited and are available from the original authors on request \citep{filbin2018, jerbyarnon2021}. The input matrices and cell annotations of all analyses in this article, and the count matrices that we derived from the realigned reads, are available from Zenodo \todo{Zenodo DOI}. Kinnex PBMC and Visium HD data sets are from PacBio (https://downloads.pacbcloud.com/public/dataset/Kinnex-single-cell-RNA/); LongBench data are GEO GSE303762 and s3://longbench-data \citep{you2025longbench}; ccRCC organoid data are Zenodo record 14697181 and ENA PRJEB73513 \citep{zajac2025ccrcc}; and CCLE copy number was obtained from cBioPortal (study ccle\_broad\_2019) \citep{cerami2012cbioportal, ghandi2019ccle}.
