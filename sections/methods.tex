\section{Methods}\label{sec:methods}

\subsection{Simulations}

Simulated tumours were generated with the inferCNV benchmarking toolkit (\texttt{simCNV.R}, https://github.com/broadinstitute/inferCNV\_benchmarking; a copy with explicit seed and output arguments is in the analysis repository) using its mean--variance method, which fits the mean--variance and mean--dropout relationships of a set of normal cells, as for the h-spike. Smart-seq2 parameters were learned from 496 non-malignant cells of synovial sarcoma \citep{jerbyarnon2021} (8,252 genes) and 10x parameters from 262 normal cells (7,210 genes) \citep{pelka2021} \todo{CONFIRM: normal colon epithelial cells, inferred from cell names}; genes were placed with GENCODE v19 coordinates. Each data set contained 100 simulated normal cells, used as the reference, and tumour cells carrying one to four CNA specifications. Specification 1: chr1 1.5× and chr4 0.5× (whole chromosomes), chr2:10--50 Mb 2× and chr8:10--30 Mb 0.01×. Specification 2 adds chr10 2×; specification 3 adds to specification 2 chr17 0.01× and chr19 0.5×; specification 4 adds to specification 2 chr12 1.5× and chr13 2×. The scenarios were sim1, specification 1 (100 cells); sim2, specifications 1--2 (60 and 40 cells); sim3, specifications 1--3 (100, 60 and 40 cells); and sim4, specifications 1--4 (100, 60, 40 and 30 cells). Each scenario was simulated and analysed with seeds 1--5.

inferCNV was run with \texttt{cutoff\ =\ 1} (Smart-seq2) or 0.1 (10x), \texttt{cluster\_by\_groups\ =\ TRUE}, \texttt{analysis\_mode\ =\ "subclusters"} (Leiden), \texttt{HMM\ =\ TRUE} with \texttt{HMM\_type\ =\ "i6"} or \texttt{"i3"}, \texttt{BayesMaxPNormal\ =\ 0.5}, \texttt{denoise\ =\ TRUE} and 4 threads, with other parameters at their defaults. The results reported are from the master v1.23.0 code, with R's random-number generator set to L'Ecuyer-CMRG and seeded before \texttt{run()}; runs with the release build using \texttt{seed} agreed within 0.006 on every metric. Calls were scored per cell and gene: the expected state of each gene in each cell came from the specification of the cell's simulated group (neutral for normal cells), and the predicted state from the HMM region covering the gene in the cell's subcluster. Simulated fold changes 0.01, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2 and 3 map to i6 states 1--6; for i3, losses, neutral and gains map to states 1--3. Sensitivity is the fraction of altered positions in tumour cells called in the correct direction; precision the fraction of non-neutral calls in the correct direction; exact-state accuracy the fraction of altered positions assigned the exact i6 state; and the reference call rate the fraction of non-neutral calls in reference cells. Purity is the fraction of tumour cells in subclusters whose majority belongs to their own simulated group.

\subsection{Evaluation against DNA-based copy number}

Data were obtained as in \citet{schmid2025}. MM199 counts were taken from GEO GSE141299 (GSM4200471) \citep{mahdipourshirayeh2022scicnv}. Because the reference annotation used by Schmid et al.~was not deposited, we repeated their cell filtering (more than 3,000 counts, less than 5\% mitochondrial reads) and Louvain clustering (resolution 0.5, 10 principal components) in Seurat, using log normalisation instead of scran. Clusters of plasma cells expressing IgG-λ markers were labelled malignant (1,916 cells), and clusters of B cells and NK/T cells formed the reference (245 cells). BCC06 and BCC06post counts and annotations were taken from GEO GSE123813 \citep{yost2019}; as in Schmid et al., endothelial cells, melanocytes and myofibroblasts of patient su006 formed the reference (33 cells pre-treatment, 280 post-treatment) and the two tumour clusters were the observations (1,403 and 320 cells). Gene positions were from GENCODE v47 (GRCh38), made with \texttt{gtf\_to\_position\_file.py} and restricted to chr1--22, X and Y. Whole-exome copy number segments (GATK) were taken from the Schmid et al.~Zenodo deposit \citep{schmid2025zenodoTruth}.

inferCNV was run with \texttt{cutoff\ =\ 0.1}, \texttt{cluster\_by\_groups\ =\ TRUE}, \texttt{denoise\ =\ TRUE}, \texttt{HMM\ =\ TRUE}, \texttt{HMM\_type\ =\ "i6"}, \texttt{analysis\_mode\ =\ "subclusters"}, \texttt{BayesMaxPNormal\ =\ 0.5}, \texttt{write\_expr\_matrix\ =\ TRUE}, \texttt{seed\ =\ 1} and 4 threads, as in Schmid et al.~except for the seed and thread count. After filtering, 5,740 (MM199), 6,963 (BCC06) and 7,401 (BCC06post) genes remained.

The evaluation re-implements that of Schmid et al.~Truth segments were tiled into 100-kb bins, each bin was assigned the mean of its segment calls (loss = 1, neutral = 2, gain = 3), and bins were called losses below 1.5 and gains above 2.5. The CNV score of a gene was the mean over malignant cells of its filtered i6 state, with states 0 and 0.5 mapped to 0, state 1 to 1, and states 1.5, 2 and 3 to 2, plus 1; the expression score was the per-gene mean of the final residual expression plus 1. Bin scores were the mean over overlapping genes, and only bins covered by inferCNV genes were scored (Schmid et al.~used bins covered by all callers). Metrics were Pearson correlation with the truth, AUC for gains and for losses, partial AUC (sensitivity range from 0 to the sensitivity at score 2) and the maximal unweighted three-class F1 over a grid of loss and gain thresholds, with sensitivity and precision at the optimum. Our re-implementation of the partial AUC reproduced the BCC06 expression-score value reported by Schmid et al.~(0.523). Values for inferCNV v1.10 were taken from the source data of \citet{schmid2025}.

\subsection{Run time}

Run times for Fig. 4 were measured on a compute cluster with 4 threads per job (wall time and peak resident memory as reported by the scheduler). TNBC1 (10x, 1,097 cells: 302 normal cells as reference and two tumour clones) \citep{gao2021copykat, huang2024xclone, xclonedata} was run in expression-only mode with the Fig. 4 settings on an 8-core host with load averages of about 40, so its times are upper bounds; step times were taken from the run log. In an earlier run, Leiden subclustering appeared to take 1.6 h; this was caused by calling \texttt{run()} through \texttt{do.call()} with the object inlined in the call, which Seurat's \texttt{ScaleData} then deparses. Calling \texttt{run()} directly avoids this. For the scaling experiment (Fig.~\ref{fig:6}b--d), nested down-samples of 500, 1,000, 2,500, 5,000, 10,000 and 20,000 cells were drawn from a 10x breast cancer atlas \citep{wu2021breast} (GEO GSE176078; author cell-type labels): 80\% malignant epithelial cells from the eight tumours with the most such cells, annotated by tumour, and 20\% endothelial cells and cancer-associated fibroblasts as two reference groups, allocated proportionally, with each subset containing the smaller ones. About 8,000 genes passed \texttt{cutoff = 0.1} at every size. Each size was run with i6 and the Bayesian filter (default), i6 without it (\texttt{BayesMaxPNormal = 0}) and i3 without it, with 4 threads on lightly loaded 128-core cluster nodes. Wall time and peak resident memory were taken from \texttt{/usr/bin/time -v}, and step times from the run log.

\subsection{Reprocessing of Smart-seq2 data}

For the H3K27M glioma BCH869 and BCH1126 \citep{filbin2018} and the synovial sarcomas SS1, SS13 and SS16 \citep{jerbyarnon2021} (2,358 cells), reads were realigned to GRCh38 with GENCODE v44 annotation (CTAT genome library). Each cell was processed with STAR-Fusion 1.15.1 \citep{haas2019starfusion}, which runs STAR \citep{dobin2013star} once to produce both the genome alignment and fusion predictions; gene counts were obtained with featureCounts (Subread 2.1.1) in paired-end mode. SS18--SSX was considered detected in a cell when STAR-Fusion reported SS18 fused to any SSX paralog. Our counts agreed with the published expression values of the synovial sarcoma cells (per-cell Pearson correlation of log-scaled values, median 0.90--0.92 by patient), and SS18--SSX was detected in 71\% of the cells the original study reported as fusion-positive, with the expected SSX1 or SSX2 partner for each patient and in one of 560 immune and stromal cells. For the synovial sarcoma comparison, each cell's CNV score was the mean squared deviation of its final residuals from 1, its correlation was with the mean residual profile of the 10\% highest-scoring observed cells of the patient, and a cell was classified malignant if either value exceeded the 99th percentile of the patient's reference cells. For BCH869, published clone labels and per-clone copy number were taken from \citet{huang2024xclone}; cell names were matched between data sets (all 492 cells), truth was assigned to genes by their hg19 coordinates, and scores were compared at gene and chromosome-arm level as in the DNA-truth evaluation above.
