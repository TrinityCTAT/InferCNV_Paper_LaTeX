\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

inferCNV infers large-scale CNAs from single-cell and spatial transcriptomes by comparing the smoothed expression of cells of interest with that of normal reference cells, assigning discrete copy number states with a hidden Markov model calibrated by simulation, and assessing these states with a Bayesian model. This description of the algorithm and of the release that implements it documents what the code does, so that results obtained with it can be interpreted and cited. Our analyses show that the accuracy of inferCNV depends above all on the choice of reference cells, that calls made for subclusters are more reliable than calls for single cells, and that the method applies without change to long-read single-cell and spatial data.
