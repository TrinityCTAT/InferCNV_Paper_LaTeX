\begin{figure}[p]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figures/figS_bayes.png}
\caption{\textbf{Bayesian filtering of HMM calls (extdata oligodendroglioma example).} \textbf{a}, The latent mixture model fitted to each predicted CNV region in each subcluster. The residuals $x_{ij}$ of the region's genes in each cell are normal with the mean $\mu_k$ and precision $\tau_k$ of the cell's state $\varepsilon_j$, which are fixed from the h-spike. Cell states are drawn from mixing proportions $\theta$ with a flat Dirichlet prior. P(normal) is the posterior mean of $\theta$ for the neutral state. \textbf{b}, Posterior distributions of $\theta$ (6 chains × 1,000 draws) for six MGH36 regions, from confident (1p, 1 copy; 7q, 4 copies) to marginal (15q, 3 copies, P(normal) = 0.48). \textbf{c}, 1 $-$ P(normal) for the 72 regions called by the i6 HMM, along the genome (one row per Leiden subcluster, one per tumour). \textbf{d}, P(normal) of each region, coloured by HMM state. Lines mark the BayesMaxPNormal values in \textbf{e--f}. Triangles: regions whose most probable state is neutral, which \texttt{reassignCNVs} resets to neutral (2 copies) even when P(normal) is below the threshold. \textbf{e}, i6 calls after filtering with BayesMaxPNormal = 0 (model off), 0.5 (default; 0.8 gives the same calls), 0.3 and 0.2. \textbf{f}, Number of non-neutral regions retained as a function of BayesMaxPNormal.}
\label{fig:S2}
\end{figure}
